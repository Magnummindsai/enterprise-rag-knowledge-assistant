# Expense Reimbursement Policy

> Synthetic document for demonstration purposes. Northwind Dynamics is a fictional company.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

### 1.1 Purpose
This policy explains which business expenses Northwind Dynamics will reimburse and how to claim them. Employees are expected to spend company money as carefully as if it were their own.

### 1.2 Scope
This policy applies to all employees. Travel booking rules are defined in the Business Travel Policy (TRV); this policy governs how those costs are claimed.

## 2. Submitting Claims

### 2.1 Expense System
All claims must be submitted through the Expensify system. Paper claims are not accepted.

### 2.2 Submission Deadline
Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Claims submitted between 31 and 90 days late require department head approval. Claims older than 90 days will not be reimbursed.

### 2.3 Receipts
An itemised receipt is required for every expense of USD 25 or more. For expenses under USD 25, a description of the expense is sufficient. Credit card statements are not accepted as receipts. If a receipt is lost, the employee must complete a Missing Receipt Declaration; no more than 2 declarations are permitted per calendar year.

### 2.4 Reimbursement Timing
Approved claims are reimbursed with the next payroll run, provided approval is completed at least 5 working days before the payroll cut-off date.

## 3. Approval

### 3.1 Approval Levels
Every claim must be approved by the claimant's line manager. Claims above USD 1,000 also require approval by the department head. Claims above USD 5,000 also require approval by the Finance Business Partner. Approvers may not approve their own expenses or expenses from which they directly benefited.

### 3.2 Senior Claims
Expense claims from Vice Presidents and above are approved by the Chief Financial Officer's office.

## 4. Corporate Card

### 4.1 Eligibility
Employees who travel more than 4 times per year or regularly incur business expenses are eligible for a corporate card. The standard monthly limit is USD 5,000.

### 4.2 Use
The corporate card must be used for business expenses only. Personal use is prohibited, even if repaid. Cash withdrawals are permitted only while travelling internationally, up to USD 200 per trip.

### 4.3 Reconciliation
Corporate card transactions must be reconciled in Expensify with receipts within 30 days of the statement date. Repeated failure to reconcile may lead to suspension of the card.

## 5. Allowable Expenses

### 5.1 Meals While Travelling
Meals while travelling are claimed using the per diem rates in the Business Travel Policy (TRV section 6.1). Itemised meal claims are only allowed where a per diem is not claimed.

### 5.2 Client Entertainment
Client entertainment is reimbursable up to USD 120 per person, including the employee, per event. The names and organisations of all attendees must be listed. Entertainment of government officials requires prior approval from the Legal and Compliance team.

### 5.3 Team Meals
Team celebration meals are limited to USD 50 per person and require department head approval in advance. The most senior employee present must pay and claim.

### 5.4 Alcohol
Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of client entertainment within the per-person limit, and never on its own.

### 5.5 Home Office and Internet
Remote-eligible employees may claim internet costs of up to USD 40 per month. Home office equipment is covered by the Remote Work & Equipment Policy (RWE).

### 5.6 Training and Books
Professional books and online courses under USD 300 may be claimed with manager approval. Training above USD 300 must be requested through the Learning & Development team.

### 5.7 Mobile Phones
Employees with a business need who use a personal mobile phone may claim up to USD 50 per month towards their phone bill.

## 6. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

### 6.1 Examples
The following are not reimbursable: personal travel insurance; traffic, parking or speeding fines; personal grooming; in-room movies and minibar items; gym or spa fees; upgrades not permitted by the Travel Policy; costs for accompanying family members; lost or stolen personal property; and gifts to colleagues.

## 7. Compliance

### 7.1 Audits
Finance audits a random sample of at least 5% of claims each month.

### 7.2 Misuse
Submitting a false or inflated claim is gross misconduct and may result in dismissal. Overpayments will be recovered through payroll.
