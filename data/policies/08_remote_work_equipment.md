# Remote Work & Equipment Policy

> Synthetic document for demonstration purposes. Northwind Dynamics is a fictional company.

## 1. Purpose and Eligibility

### 1.1 Purpose
Northwind Dynamics operates a hybrid working model. This policy explains how remote work is arranged and what equipment and allowances are provided.

### 1.2 Hybrid Model
Employees in hybrid roles are expected to work from their assigned office at least 2 days per week, including the team's designated anchor day. Fully remote roles are designated as such in the employment contract.

### 1.3 Eligibility
Remote work eligibility depends on role requirements. Employees in probation (first 3 months) are expected to work from the office at least 3 days per week.

## 2. Working Location

### 2.1 Home Working Environment
Employees working remotely must have a safe, private workspace and a reliable internet connection of at least 25 Mbps download. Confidential conversations must not be held where they can be overheard.

### 2.2 Working From Another Country
Working from a country other than the employee's country of employment is limited to 20 working days per calendar year and requires approval from the manager and People Operations at least 15 working days in advance, due to tax and immigration implications. Some countries are excluded; see the intranet list.

### 2.3 Core Hours
Remote employees must be available during core hours of 10:00 to 15:00 local office time, unless otherwise agreed with their manager.

## 3. Equipment

### 3.1 Standard Equipment
All employees receive a company laptop. Hybrid and remote employees may also request one external monitor, a keyboard, a mouse and a headset through the IT Service Desk.

### 3.2 Home Office Allowance
Hybrid and fully remote employees receive a one-time home office setup allowance of USD 500 to purchase ergonomic furniture such as a desk or chair. Claims must be submitted with receipts through Expensify within 6 months of joining or of becoming remote-eligible. A refresh of USD 250 is available every 3 years.

### 3.3 Internet Costs
Internet costs are claimable up to USD 40 per month, as set out in the Expense Reimbursement Policy (EXP section 5.5).

### 3.4 Care of Equipment
Company equipment remains company property. Employees must take reasonable care of it and report loss or theft to the IT Service Desk and, for security reasons, to the Security Operations Centre within 1 hour (see ITS section 6.2).

### 3.5 Returning Equipment
On leaving the company, all company equipment must be returned within 10 working days. Equipment purchased with the home office allowance does not need to be returned.

## 4. Health, Safety and Wellbeing

### 4.1 Workstation Assessment
Remote and hybrid employees must complete an online workstation self-assessment within their first month and whenever they move home.

### 4.2 Right to Disconnect
Employees are not expected to respond to non-urgent messages outside their working hours. Managers should use scheduled send for messages outside core hours.

## 5. Changes and Review

### 5.1 Changing Arrangements
Either the employee or the manager may request a review of remote working arrangements with 4 weeks' notice.

### 5.2 Review
This policy is reviewed annually by People Operations.
