# Procurement & Purchasing Policy

> Synthetic document for demonstration purposes. Northwind Dynamics is a fictional company.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

### 1.1 Purpose
This policy ensures that Northwind Dynamics buys goods and services in a way that delivers value for money, manages risk and complies with the law.

### 1.2 Scope
This policy applies to all purchases of goods and services by any employee, regardless of how they are paid. Individual employee expenses are covered by the Expense Reimbursement Policy (EXP).

## 2. Purchasing Thresholds

### 2.1 Competitive Quotes
The number of quotes required depends on the total contract value (including all years and extensions): under USD 10,000 requires 1 quote; USD 10,000 to USD 50,000 requires at least 3 written quotes; USD 50,000 to USD 250,000 requires a formal Request for Proposal (RFP) managed by Procurement; above USD 250,000 requires a formal RFP and approval by the Procurement Committee.

### 2.2 Approval Authority
Purchase approval limits are: line managers up to USD 10,000; department heads up to USD 50,000; Vice Presidents up to USD 250,000; the Chief Financial Officer up to USD 1,000,000; the Chief Executive Officer above USD 1,000,000.

### 2.3 Splitting Purchases
Splitting a purchase into smaller orders to avoid a threshold is prohibited and will be treated as a policy breach.

## 3. Purchase Orders

### 3.1 No PO, No Pay
A purchase order (PO) must be raised in the procurement system (BuyRight) and approved before a supplier is engaged or goods are ordered. Invoices without a valid PO number will be returned to the supplier unpaid.

### 3.2 Exceptions
POs are not required for: purchases under USD 500 made on a corporate card; utilities; and statutory payments such as taxes.

### 3.3 Emergency Purchases
In a genuine emergency affecting safety or business continuity, a purchase may be made without prior PO, provided a retrospective PO is raised within 2 working days with a written justification.

## 4. Suppliers

### 4.1 Preferred Suppliers
Where a preferred supplier agreement exists for a category (for example laptops, office supplies or travel), the preferred supplier must be used unless Procurement approves an exception.

### 4.2 Supplier Onboarding
New suppliers must be onboarded through Procurement before a PO is issued. Onboarding includes sanctions screening, financial checks, bank detail verification and, where the supplier will access company data, a security assessment by Information Security.

### 4.3 Supplier Security Assessment
Any supplier that will store or process Confidential or Restricted data (as defined in the IT Acceptable Use & Security Policy, ITS section 2.1) must pass a security assessment and sign a Data Processing Agreement before contract signature.

### 4.4 Bank Detail Changes
Requests from suppliers to change bank details must be verified by phone using contact details already held on file, never those provided in the request.

## 5. Contracts

### 5.1 Signing Authority
Only employees with delegated signing authority may sign contracts. Contracts above USD 50,000, or any contract with non-standard liability, indemnity or data protection terms, must be reviewed by Legal before signature.

### 5.2 Software and SaaS
All software and SaaS purchases, including free trials that require accepting terms, must be approved by IT and Information Security before use, regardless of value.

### 5.3 Auto-Renewals
Contract owners must record renewal dates in BuyRight and review auto-renewing contracts at least 90 days before the renewal date.

## 6. Ethics and Conflicts of Interest

### 6.1 Conflicts of Interest
Employees must declare any personal relationship or financial interest in a supplier and must not take part in selecting or managing that supplier.

### 6.2 Gifts and Hospitality
Employees involved in procurement must not accept gifts or hospitality from current or prospective suppliers during an active tender. Outside a tender, gifts valued above USD 75 must be declared in the gifts register.

### 6.3 Sustainability
For purchases above USD 50,000, supplier evaluation must include environmental and social criteria weighted at a minimum of 10% of the total score.
