# IT Acceptable Use & Security Policy

> Synthetic document for demonstration purposes. Northwind Dynamics is a fictional company.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

### 1.1 Purpose
This policy defines acceptable use of Northwind Dynamics information systems and the security behaviours expected of every user, in order to protect company, customer and employee data.

### 1.2 Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors and third parties who use company devices, networks, applications or data. Password and access requirements are detailed in the Password & Access Control Standard (PAC).

## 2. Data Classification

### 2.1 Classification Levels
All information must be classified as one of: Public (approved for external release); Internal (default for business information); Confidential (customer data, financial results, HR data, contracts); or Restricted (credentials, encryption keys, payment card data, special category personal data).

### 2.2 Handling Rules
Confidential and Restricted data must be encrypted in transit and at rest. Restricted data must never be sent by email, even internally, and must only be shared through the approved secure vault. Confidential data may be emailed internally but must be sent to external parties only via the approved secure file transfer service.

## 3. Acceptable Use

### 3.1 Company Devices
Company devices are provided for business purposes. Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work, consume excessive resources or breach any policy. Installing software not listed in the Company Portal requires an IT Service Desk request and security review.

### 3.2 Prohibited Activities
Users must not: disable or bypass endpoint security tools; share accounts; connect unauthorised devices to the corporate network; use company systems for illegal content, harassment or personal commercial activity; or store company data on personal cloud storage.

### 3.3 Generative AI Tools
Only generative AI tools approved by Information Security (listed on the intranet AI page) may be used for company work. Confidential or Restricted data must never be entered into public or unapproved AI tools. Outputs from AI tools must be reviewed by a person before use in customer-facing material.

### 3.4 Personal Devices (BYOD)
Personal smartphones may be used to access company email and chat only if enrolled in Mobile Device Management (MDM). MDM enforces a device passcode, encryption and remote wipe of company data. Personal laptops must not be used to access company systems.

### 3.5 Removable Media
USB storage devices are blocked by default. Exceptions require Information Security approval and must use company-issued encrypted drives.

## 4. Device Security

### 4.1 Screen Lock
Devices must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Users must lock their screens manually whenever they leave a device unattended.

### 4.2 Patching
Security updates must be installed within 7 days of release. Critical updates flagged by IT must be installed within 48 hours. Devices that are more than 14 days out of compliance will be blocked from corporate resources.

### 4.3 Travel
When travelling, devices must never be left unattended in vehicles or checked in as hold baggage. Use a privacy screen in public places. Travel to certain countries requires a loaner laptop; check with the IT Service Desk at least 10 working days before departure.

### 4.4 Public Wi-Fi
When using public or hotel Wi-Fi, users must connect to the corporate VPN before accessing any company resources.

## 5. Email and Phishing

### 5.1 Reporting Phishing
Suspicious emails must be reported using the "Report Phish" button in the email client. Do not click links, open attachments or reply to suspicious messages.

### 5.2 Phishing Simulations
Information Security runs regular phishing simulations. Users who fail 2 simulations in 6 months must complete additional training within 14 days.

### 5.3 Auto-Forwarding
Automatic forwarding of company email to external addresses is prohibited.

## 6. Security Incidents

### 6.1 What to Report
Security incidents include lost or stolen devices, suspected malware, accidental disclosure of data, suspected account compromise and physical security breaches.

### 6.2 How and When to Report
All suspected security incidents must be reported to the Security Operations Centre (SOC) within 1 hour of discovery, via security@northwind.example or the 24/7 hotline on extension 4444. Lost or stolen devices must be reported immediately so they can be remotely wiped.

### 6.3 Do Not Investigate Alone
Users must not attempt to investigate or remediate an incident themselves, as this may destroy evidence.

## 7. Training and Monitoring

### 7.1 Mandatory Training
All users must complete security awareness training within 30 days of joining and annually thereafter.

### 7.2 Monitoring
Company systems are monitored for security purposes in accordance with applicable law. Users should have no expectation of privacy for data stored on company systems.

### 7.3 Violations
Violations of this policy may result in loss of access and disciplinary action up to and including termination.
