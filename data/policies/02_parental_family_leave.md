# Parental & Family Leave Policy

> Synthetic document for demonstration purposes. Northwind Dynamics is a fictional company.

## 1. Purpose and Eligibility

### 1.1 Purpose
Northwind Dynamics supports employees as they grow and care for their families. This policy describes paid leave for birth parents, non-birth parents, adoptive parents and carers.

### 1.2 Eligibility
Employees are eligible for enhanced (company-paid) parental leave after 6 months of continuous service at the expected week of birth or placement. Employees with less than 6 months of service remain entitled to statutory leave and pay.

## 2. Birth Parent Leave

### 2.1 Entitlement
The birth parent is entitled to up to 26 weeks of parental leave. The first 16 weeks are paid at 100% of base salary. Weeks 17 to 26 are paid at 50% of base salary. Additional unpaid leave of up to 26 weeks may be requested.

### 2.2 Start of Leave
Leave may begin up to 11 weeks before the expected week of childbirth. The employee must notify HR in writing at least 15 weeks before the expected week of childbirth, stating the intended start date.

## 3. Non-Birth Parent Leave

### 3.1 Entitlement
The non-birth parent (including partners, spouses and same-sex partners) is entitled to 12 weeks of leave paid at 100% of base salary. The leave may be taken in up to 3 blocks of at least 2 weeks each within the first 12 months after birth.

### 3.2 Notice
At least 8 weeks' notice of the intended leave dates is required.

## 4. Adoption and Surrogacy Leave

### 4.1 Primary Adopter
The primary adopter, or the intended parent in a surrogacy arrangement who will be the primary carer, receives the same entitlement as the birth parent in section 2.1.

### 4.2 Secondary Adopter
The secondary adopter receives the same entitlement as the non-birth parent in section 3.1.

## 5. Keeping-in-Touch and Returning

### 5.1 Keeping-in-Touch Days
Employees on parental leave may work up to 10 paid keeping-in-touch (KIT) days without ending their leave. KIT days are voluntary and must be agreed with the manager.

### 5.2 Phased Return
On return from parental leave of 12 weeks or more, employees may work 80% of their contracted hours at 100% pay for the first 4 weeks.

### 5.3 Annual Leave During Parental Leave
Annual leave continues to accrue during parental leave. Employees are encouraged to use accrued leave before or immediately after parental leave. The 31 March carry-over deadline in the Annual & Sick Leave Policy (LEV section 2.4) is extended by 6 months for leave accrued during parental leave.

## 6. Family and Carer Leave

### 6.1 Carer's Leave
Employees may take up to 5 days of paid carer's leave per year to care for a dependant with a long-term care need.

### 6.2 Emergency Dependant Leave
Employees may take reasonable unpaid time off, typically 1 to 2 days, to deal with an unexpected emergency involving a dependant. The first 2 days per year are paid.

### 6.3 Fertility Treatment
Employees undergoing fertility treatment, and their partners, may take up to 5 days of paid leave per year for appointments.
