# Annual & Sick Leave Policy

> Synthetic document for demonstration purposes. Northwind Dynamics is a fictional company.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

### 1.1 Purpose
This policy sets out how employees of Northwind Dynamics accrue, request and take annual leave (vacation) and sick leave. It aims to support rest and wellbeing while ensuring business continuity.

### 1.2 Scope
This policy applies to all permanent and fixed-term employees working 20 hours per week or more. Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis. Contractors and agency workers are not covered and should refer to their agency terms. Parental and family leave is covered separately in the Parental & Family Leave Policy (PFL).

## 2. Annual Leave

### 2.1 Entitlement
Full-time employees are entitled to 25 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. After 5 years of continuous service, entitlement increases to 27 days. After 10 years of continuous service, entitlement increases to 30 days.

### 2.2 Accrual
Annual leave accrues monthly at a rate of 2.08 days per month for full-time employees. New joiners accrue leave from their first day of employment. Employees may take leave in advance of accrual up to a maximum of 5 days, with manager approval.

### 2.3 Requesting Annual Leave
Leave must be requested through the HR portal (PeopleHub). Requests of up to 3 consecutive days require at least 5 working days' notice. Requests of 4 or more consecutive days require at least 20 working days' notice. Managers must approve or decline a request within 3 working days. Requests are approved on a first-come, first-served basis where team coverage is a constraint.

### 2.4 Carry-Over
Employees may carry over up to 5 unused days of annual leave into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March of the following year, after which they are forfeited. Carry-over beyond 5 days is only permitted in exceptional circumstances, such as extended sick leave or an operational freeze, and requires written approval from the Head of People Operations.

### 2.5 Blackout Periods
Finance team members may not take more than 2 consecutive days of annual leave during the final 10 working days of each financial quarter. Other departments may define blackout periods of up to 10 working days per year, which must be published at least 60 days in advance.

### 2.6 Payment in Lieu
Unused annual leave is not paid out during employment. On termination, accrued but untaken leave for the current year is paid out at the employee's base daily rate. Leave taken in advance of accrual will be deducted from final pay.

## 3. Sick Leave

### 3.1 Entitlement
Employees receive up to 10 days of fully paid sick leave per calendar year. After 10 days, a further 20 days are paid at 50% of base salary. Unused sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out on termination.

### 3.2 Notification
Employees who are unwell must notify their line manager before their scheduled start time, or as soon as reasonably practicable, on each day of absence unless a return date has been agreed. Absences must also be recorded in PeopleHub within 2 working days of returning to work.

### 3.3 Medical Certification
Self-certification is accepted for absences of up to 3 consecutive calendar days. For absences of 4 or more consecutive calendar days, a medical certificate from a registered practitioner is required. HR may request a medical certificate for any absence where there is a pattern of frequent short-term absence (more than 4 separate occasions in a rolling 12-month period).

### 3.4 Return to Work
After any sick leave of 5 or more working days, the employee and their manager must hold a short return-to-work conversation. Where appropriate, a phased return or workplace adjustments may be agreed with Occupational Health.

### 3.5 Sickness During Annual Leave
If an employee falls ill during annual leave and provides a medical certificate, the affected days will be recorded as sick leave and the annual leave will be reinstated.

## 4. Other Leave Types

### 4.1 Bereavement Leave
Employees may take up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member (spouse, partner, child, parent, sibling) and up to 2 days for other relatives.

### 4.2 Volunteering Leave
Employees may take 2 paid days per year to volunteer with a registered charity. Requests follow the standard process in section 2.3.

### 4.3 Jury Duty
Employees summoned for jury duty receive full pay for the duration of service and must provide the court summons to HR.
