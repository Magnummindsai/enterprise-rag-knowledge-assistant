# Business Travel Policy

> Synthetic document for demonstration purposes. Northwind Dynamics is a fictional company.

## 1. Purpose and Principles

### 1.1 Purpose
This policy governs the booking and conduct of business travel. Business travel should only be undertaken when a virtual meeting is not a practical alternative. Travellers must choose the most economical option that reasonably meets business needs.

### 1.2 Related Policies
Claiming travel costs is covered by the Expense Reimbursement Policy (EXP). Security requirements for devices used while travelling are covered by the IT Acceptable Use & Security Policy (ITS).

## 2. Approval

### 2.1 Pre-Approval
All business travel requires pre-approval from the traveller's line manager through the TravelDesk portal before any booking is made. International travel additionally requires approval from the department head. Trips with an estimated total cost above USD 5,000 also require approval from the Finance Business Partner.

### 2.2 Booking Channel
All flights, rail and hotels must be booked through the corporate travel agency, TravelDesk. Bookings made outside TravelDesk will only be reimbursed if the traveller can show the fare was at least 10% cheaper than the lowest TravelDesk fare available at the time of booking.

### 2.3 Advance Booking
Flights must be booked at least 14 days in advance where possible. Bookings made fewer than 7 days before departure require a short justification in TravelDesk.

## 3. Air Travel

### 3.1 Class of Travel
Economy class is the standard for all flights. Premium economy is permitted for flights with a scheduled duration over 6 hours. Business class is permitted only for flights with a scheduled duration over 10 hours and requires department head approval. First class is never permitted.

### 3.2 Baggage and Seats
One checked bag is reimbursable. Seat selection fees of up to USD 40 per flight segment are reimbursable.

### 3.3 Loyalty Programmes
Employees may keep personal loyalty points earned on business travel, but loyalty programmes must not influence the choice of airline or hotel if a cheaper option is available.

## 4. Rail and Ground Transport

### 4.1 Rail
Standard class rail is the default. First class rail is permitted for journeys longer than 3 hours.

### 4.2 Taxis and Ride-Hailing
Taxis and ride-hailing are permitted where public transport is impractical, for travel between 22:00 and 06:00, or when carrying heavy equipment.

### 4.3 Car Rental
Car rental is permitted when it is cheaper than alternatives or when public transport is unavailable. The maximum category is intermediate (compact SUV or equivalent). Collision damage waiver insurance is included in corporate agreements and must not be purchased separately.

### 4.4 Personal Vehicles
Use of a personal vehicle for business travel is reimbursed at USD 0.60 per mile (USD 0.37 per km). Commuting between home and the employee's regular office is not reimbursable.

## 5. Accommodation

### 5.1 Hotel Rate Caps
Hotel room rates, excluding taxes, must not exceed the following nightly caps: USD 250 in Tier 1 cities (New York, London, San Francisco, Zurich, Tokyo, Singapore); USD 180 in Tier 2 cities (all other capital cities and major metro areas); USD 130 in all other locations. Stays above the cap require department head approval in TravelDesk before booking.

### 5.2 Extended Stays
For stays of 7 nights or more at one location, serviced apartments should be considered and may be booked when cheaper than a hotel.

## 6. Per Diem and Meals

### 6.1 Per Diem Rates
Travellers staying overnight may claim a meal per diem instead of itemised meal receipts. The daily per diem is USD 75 for domestic travel and USD 95 for international travel. On the first and last day of travel, 75% of the daily rate applies.

### 6.2 Provided Meals
If a meal is provided (for example at a conference or by a client), the per diem is reduced by 20% for breakfast, 30% for lunch and 50% for dinner.

## 7. Safety and Duty of Care

### 7.1 High-Risk Destinations
Travel to destinations rated High Risk or above by the corporate security provider requires approval from the Chief Operating Officer and completion of a travel security briefing.

### 7.2 Travel Insurance
All business travellers are covered by the corporate travel insurance policy. Personal travel insurance is not reimbursable.

### 7.3 Combining Business and Leisure
Employees may extend business trips for personal leisure, provided that any additional costs (extra nights, changed flights) are paid personally and the leisure days are recorded as annual leave.
